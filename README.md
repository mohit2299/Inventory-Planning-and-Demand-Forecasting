Reebok faces significant challenges in inventory planning and demand forecasting for NFL replica jerseys due to highly unpredictable player and team performance throughout the season.

The company must determine the optimal balance between pre-season inventory planning and in-season replenishment ("The Chase") while considering:

Uncertain consumer demand based on player popularity and team success.
Long supply chain lead times (offshore manufacturing, shipping delays).
Retailer expectations for short lead-time fulfillment, particularly for hot-market items.
Minimizing leftover inventory at the end of the season while ensuring enough stock to maximize profits.
Deciding between producing "dressed" jerseys (player-specific) vs. "blank" jerseys (printed on demand).
Key Questions:
How should Reebok optimize inventory planning despite uncertainty?
Should the company prioritize minimizing leftover inventory or maximizing profits?
What is the cost tradeoff between overstocking (excess inventory) vs. understocking (missed sales opportunities)?
How can Reebok's supply chain be more responsive to in-season demand shifts?
How does the Newsvendor Model apply to inventory decisions?
Goal:
To develop a data-driven approach for inventory planning that reduces risk, improves forecasting, and ensures an optimal balance between demand fulfillment and excess inventory costs.
